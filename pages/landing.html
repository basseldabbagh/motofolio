My MotoFolio is a universal vehicle maintenance tracker, periodic service interval calculator, workshop expense ledger, and printable A4 condition report builder. No sign-ups, no servers — your garage stays on your device, with optional private sync to your own Google Drive.

Everything You Need to Maintain Any Vehicle

Built for car owners, DIY mechanics, enthusiasts, and small fleets — fast, structured, and completely private.

Periodic Service Interval Engine

Automatically calculates remaining distance and time for fluids, filters, belts, and brakes, flagging items as Good, Due Soon, or Overdue.

Workshop Expense Ledger

Log parts vs. labor costs, mechanic workshops, and payment methods with instant search, date/category filters, and interactive SVG charts.

Fuel Efficiency Tracker

Record fill-ups, track L/100km or km/L economy trends, and monitor total fuel spending alongside your maintenance costs.

Printable A4 Reports

Generate clean, print-ready A4 Vehicle Condition and Service History reports in one click — ideal for resale or workshop handovers.

CSV, JSON & Google Drive

Export and import spreadsheets (.CSV) or full JSON v2.0 backups anytime, or sync directly to a private folder in your Google Drive.

Zero-Server Privacy & PWA

Installable on desktop and mobile. Your vehicle data lives in browser storage and never passes through third-party tracking servers.
